# A Bestiary of Azul · Plate I · The Gold Wyvern — treatment

Style: **Copperplate Engraving** (Lemo-Opuscar). New series: *A Bestiary of Azul*, a mage-naturalist's
field notes on the creatures of Azul. Format: 1080×1920 portrait plate, 24 fps, 141.2 s. Narrator Kokoro
`bm_george` (sped 5 %, pitch kept), subtitles on a script slip + `subtitles_en.srt`. Script approved by the
user; line and word timings in `timeline.json` and `assets/words_film.json`.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A book of plates.** Five small plates laid on the bed one after another, one per section (comparison,
   anatomy, jaws, insulation, warning). Clear, but five separate pictures of one animal never add up to a
   single specimen, and new sheets every 25 s read as slides.
2. **One plate, dissected (chosen).** One portrait plate grows for the whole film: the comparison band at
   the top, the Gold Wyvern in the centre, magnification roundels in the margins for the Dynamo Sacs, the
   skull and the Static-Ticks, and the Guild's doctrine in a cartouche at the foot. The loupe visits each
   part in teaching order; colour arrives only on the part being taught; the last shot pulls back to the
   finished plate with every label in place. It is exactly what a mage's field-note plate would be.
3. **States of the plate.** One view of the wyvern burnished and re-cut deeper each time (skin → muscle →
   organs). Native and elegant, but the comparison, the gizzard stones and the doctrine have no place in it.

**Why 2:** the script is a dissection in teaching order, and the payoff (aim at the sac) needs the whole
animal and its anatomy on one sheet. The plate is also the series' format: every episode is the next plate.

- **Opening image:** the loupe on the true dragon of fig. 1, the burin finishing its last hatching; gold
  wash blooms on the rune-carved chest on "magical" (a colour event in the first four seconds). The wyvern
  beside it stays bare paper.
- **Ending:** a log-zoom pull-out to the finished plate, the only red on the sheet a madder wash on the
  Dynamo Sac the Guild says to pierce; held, locked.
- **Score shape:** dry harpsichord while lines are cut, a cello that breathes only when colour arrives, glass
  harmonica for the cold electric moments; two real silences (before the arc, before the red).

## 2. Logline and arc

A mage's plate shows that the Gold Wyvern's lightning is anatomy, not magic, and so ends on the one place
an arrow can kill it.

- **Setup:** dragons are magical, wyverns are not; yet this one hunts with lightning.
- **Turn:** the lightning is built from flight (Dynamo Sacs), minerals (fangs, crystals) and partners
  (Static-Ticks): a power plant, which is why wards are useless.
- **Ending:** the Guild's doctrine; the sac turns red: aim here.

## 3. Benchmark

- ***Life in the Undergrowth* (BBC, 2005).** Learn: one behaviour per sequence, macro to whole animal,
  calm authority, facts arriving at the speed of looking. Don't take: music, shots, narration.
- **Medieval bestiaries** (the habit of pairing each creature with a warning or lesson). Learn: the
  creature–warning structure, marginal figures. Don't take: any image, border or text.

## 4. Look

- **The sheet is the world:** a portrait laid-paper sheet (1080×1920 at 1×), plate mark and ruled border.
  Top band: series line A BESTIARY OF AZUL · PLATE I, title THE GOLD WYVERN, italic *Dracunculus fulgur*;
  fig. 1 (Draco verus beside D. fulgur). Centre: fig. 2, the Gold Wyvern, wings spread, a cut-away window
  over the ribcage. Margins: roundels fig. 3 (sacs), fig. 4 (skull, palate, arc), fig. 5 (scales and
  Static-Ticks, with a natural-size mite). Foot: the Guild cartouche. Credits: "Drawn from life." left,
  "Plate I." right.
- **Ink:** one warm near-black; tone only from swelling hatch lines, light from the upper left.
- **Washes** (transparent, earth and mineral): gold ochre on the dragon's runes; amber on the sacs, the
  fangs and the scales; pale yellow along the arc; blue-grey on the mites; one madder red on the target sac.
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda roman capitals (title, labels, cut glyph by glyph), Bodoni italic for the Latin
  name, Pinyon Script for notes and the subtitle slip.

## 7. Cue map

- **Tempo 75 BPM** (beat 0.8 s, eighth 0.4 s), grid anchored at 0.1 s: every hit above sits on an eighth
  (0.1 + 0.4·k s); `cues.py` checks picture against score.
- **Sections:** I comparison 0–27.0 (harpsichord arpeggios, sparse; cello drone under the gold wash);
  II engine 27.0–55.5 (harpsichord ostinato in eighths, a pulse on each downstroke, cello swell at 51.7);
  III spark 55.5–84.5 (harpsichord, thinning; silence 80.0–81.3; glass harmonica on the arc);
  IV insulation 84.5–108.8 (harpsichord pizzicato-like, glass-harmonica tings on the mites);
  V doctrine 108.8–141.2 (harpsichord in a firm walking line for the doctrine; silence 131.9–133.3;
  low cello on the red; final cadence and a long low note).

## 9. Subtitles and titles

- **Subtitles:** Pinyon Script on a deckled paper slip, bottom centre (above the Shorts interface), at most
  two lines; one slip per spoken line, held until the next line (every hold ≥ the line + 0.6 s and ≥ 1.8 s).
- **Plate lettering:** cut glyph by glyph (roman), written in one stroke (script notes); notes stay at least
  chars ÷ 12 + 1 s. Labels: THORACIC ELECTROPLAQUES, HOLLOW COPPER-ENAMEL FANGS, PIEZOELECTRIC CRYSTALS,
  STATIC-TICK; notes: *no mana pathways*, *Dynamo Sacs*, *copper ore · quartz · Sylvan iron*, *triple
  sheaths of fat*, *nat. size*, *aim here*.

## 10. Visual details invented here (not lore)

*Draco verus* as the true dragon's plate name and runes on its chest; the wyvern's look (gold scales,
barbed tail, wing fingers); the gizzard-stone inset; the Guild cartouche, its three emblems (a dropped
sword, a stone slab, a wooden bolt) and the red "aim here" mark; the plate credits "Drawn from life." and
"Plate I.".
