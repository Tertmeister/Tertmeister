Project Icarus was never written down.
That's how you know it was real.
On paper, it was mercy.
Take the barbarian children from the northern raids.
Teach them letters.
Teach them manners.
Teach them to kneel.
The results were... varied.
Some learned to read.
Some learned to lie.
Some never spoke again.
They named it after a boy who flew too high.
No one at court asked why.
The program was quietly ended.
Every subject was accounted for.
Except one.
Now, the nobles whisper about the hero of Aquarion.
No family. No village.
No childhood anyone remembers.
Just perfect manners...
and a habit of kneeling a little too fast.
