Every culture on the continent has a story about the Great Oak.
Some pray to it.
Some marry beneath it.
Some bury their dead in its shade.
None of them agree on who planted it.
Last spring, scholars from the Royal Archives went digging.
Not at the tree.
Around it.
They found seashells on mountaintops.
Ancient riverbeds running uphill.
Layers of rock stacked in the wrong order.
Their conclusion: the continent was once upside down.
Something turned it over.
Whether the Oak had anything to do with it... is still debated.
The clerics who tend it don't debate.
They just won't let anyone near the roots.
Some of them say the tree isn't growing.
It's holding something down.
And whatever is under there... has been very patient.
