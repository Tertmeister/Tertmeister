This continent runs on legends.
Some are argued over fondly in the town square.
Others you only whisper in taverns, low enough that the inquisitors don't hear.
But none of them unsettle historians quite like Lady Jane.
When the Queen of Aquarion made part of the royal records public, scholars expected ledgers.
They found a name.
No house. No birth. No death.
Just "Lady Jane," in the margins of three centuries of history.
Some say she's a spirit.
Some say she's an entity.
Some say she's the Black Witch.
Others call her the Witch of Hope.
Which name you use... depends on what she did for you.
