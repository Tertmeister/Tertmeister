# Ep. 6 · The Bifrost — treatment

Style: **Halftone Dossier** (Lemo-Opuscar). Series: *What the Songs Don't Tell You*.
Format: 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 64.3 s. English voice (Kokoro `am_michael`, the series narrator),
burned-in caption bar + `subtitles_en.srt`. Script approved by the user; timings in `timeline.json`.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The declassified report.** One long report page; black bars lift one by one until the question
   at the end shows. Strong on secrecy, but one static page for 64 s, and the tunnels, the wall and the
   thieves never get pictures of their own.
2. **Numbered exhibits (chosen).** Cicada's own case file on the Bifrost, read out one exhibit at a time:
   the roads, the false wall, Cicada itself, the one infiltration, Agent Kat's report, the captured
   thieves' statements. The verdict is a stamp, and the last page is an open question whose answer stays
   redacted. It follows the script's order exactly, gives each fact its own picture, and lets the
   style's native moves (exhibit numerals, mugshot, stamp, redaction) each do one job.
3. **The evidence board.** One pinned board, the camera panning from exhibit to exhibit along red string.
   Good connective feel, but in a vertical frame the board is either tiny or a long scroll, and it
   leaves no room for the secret, dark-ground underground scenes.

**Why 2:** the topic is literally an intelligence file, and the turn (a password nobody should need) is
best delivered as the file's own verdict: stamped, then left unsolved.

- **Opening image:** a manila folder on paper; a red **CLASSIFIED** stamp slams before the first word
  (hook in the first half second).
- **Ending:** the final page on dark ink: **WHO IS THE PASSWORD FOR?**, an "ANSWER:" line that stays
  blacked out, and a second stamp, **UNSOLVED**, bookending the first.
- **Score shape:** a sneaking pizzicato-and-snaps groove that drops to bass and snaps underground, swells
  once (the sanctuary), empties to silence before the verdict stamp, and ends on a bare bass pulse.

## 2. Logline and arc

Cicada's file on the thieves' secret roads proves one infiltration happened, and that the thieves guard
their sanctuaries with a password even though no outsider is supposed to know the way.

- **Setup:** the thieves' guild has its own underground roads, the Bifrost; every road hides a false wall
  and a sanctuary.
- **Turn:** Cicada failed for years; one agent, Kat, got in and reported a challenge and a password at
  every station.
- **Ending:** the thieves call it extra security; Cicada stamps it UNCONVINCED and leaves the question
  open: if only thieves know the way in, who is the password for?

## 3. Benchmark

- ***Catch Me If You Can* (2002) opening titles.** Learn: flat graphic cat-and-mouse staging, figures
  slipping through architectural frames, transitions made from the graphics, a light-footed sneaking
  rhythm. Don't take: its characters, music, colours, type or any shot.
- **The spy-film dossier briefing** (photos dropping onto a file, a name, one line of intel, a redaction).
  Learn: one exhibit per beat, a name plate and a single fact per card. Don't take: any franchise's
  music, logos or theme.

## 4. Look

- **Inks (four plus paper):** manila paper `#EAD9B4`; dark ink sepia-black `#2B1D14` (outlines, text,
  caption bar, fades); overprint shadow deep teal `#1F5C61`; highlight mustard `#E3A62B` (keywords,
  numerals); alarm vermilion `#D4381E` (stamps only). The Bifrost's routes are drawn in teal, mustard and
  vermilion: a three-ink "rainbow bridge" under the city.
- **Secret scenes** (the false wall, the final page) print on a dark-ink ground with a lighter teal dot
  layer, still multiplied by the paper.
- **Characters** (heavy 7 px outline, flat fills, glossy eyes, line boil at 10 fps): the Cicada emblem
  (a stocky insect in mustard and teal); three captured thieves (hooded, masked, eyes that dart); Agent
  Kat only as a silhouette with a redaction bar across the eyes (no face, no gender, nothing the lore
  hasn't said).
- **Type:** headlines in a black slab serif (Alfa Slab One) with a +8/+8 teal misregistration shadow;
  names and numerals in a fat rounded display face (Bagel Fat One); case numbers and HUD in a heavy
  monospace (JetBrains Mono ExtraBold); caption bar and stamps in a black-weight sans (Archivo Black).
- **HUD:** top left `CICADA · FILE 06` and an exhibit chip; top right a blinking REC dot and
  `THE BIFROST`. Inverted on dark pages. Everything sits inside the Shorts safe area (x 60–960,
  y 150–1560); the bottom of the frame is paper.

## 7. Cue map

- **Tempo 100 BPM** (beat 0.6 s, eighth 0.3 s). The grid is anchored so the verdict stamp UNCONVINCED
  (54.35 s) is a bar downbeat: bars start at 1.55 + 2.4·n s; every visual hit above sits on an eighth
  (0.35 + 0.3·k s), so picture and score share one grid (`cues.json`, checked by `cuecheck.py`).
- **Sections and instruments:**
  - Cover (0–3.95): kick on the stamp, bass enters.
  - Roads (3.95–12.05): square 8th-note bass, pizzicato motif, finger snaps on 2 and 4, hats.
  - Underground (12.05–21.65): bass + snaps only; at 16.0 bass only; 18.35 triangle pad swell and a
    marimba arpeggio (the one warm moment).
  - Cicada (21.65–29.45): full groove (kick, snare, clap, hats, bass), marimba motif; stamps on eighths.
  - Infiltration and report (29.45–47.15): pad + pizzicato, half-time; snare roll and rising sweep
    38.45 → 40.85; hits at 42.35 and 44.45.
  - Statements (47.15–53.15): groove with claps; empties at 53.15; the stamp at 54.35 lands alone;
    bass + snaps return quietly 55.0.
  - Final (58.25–64.3): bass pulse, last note on the UNSOLVED stamp, pad tail.

- Keywords: roads · Underground · Unmapped · Bifrost · wall · sanctuary · Cicada · one · Kat · odd ·

## 10. Visual details invented here (not lore)

File number 06, the Cicada emblem as an insect, the three-ink routes, FAILED entry slips, the redacted
password and answer, and the thieves' hoods and masks are picture design, not new facts about the world.
